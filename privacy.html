Privacy

LAST UPDATED: OCTOBER 2026

What is stored

Reading needs nothing from you. If you use the chat button, we keep your name, email address, and message so staff can reply. Staff logins keep an email address and a login record. Page views are counted in total only, never tied to you.

Why it is stored

Messages exist to answer you. Login records exist to keep staff accounts safe. Counts exist to learn which stories readers love. Nothing is sold, rented, or shared with ad companies.

Who sees it

Only MINIMSAAH staff. Chat messages are read by the editorial team. Security rows are read by site bosses only.

How long it is kept

Chat messages stay until deleted in the admin inbox. Security rows auto-clear after 30 days. Counts have no names at all.

Videos from other sites

Some videos play from YouTube, TikTok, Facebook, or Instagram. Those companies apply their own rules when you watch there. YouTube videos play inside this site; other providers open on their own sites.

Ask for removal

Write to minimsaah@gmail.com with the email you used, and your message or data will be removed.
